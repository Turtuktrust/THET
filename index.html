About THET

The Turtuk Health and Education Trust (THET) was established on 28th April 2011 with a noble mission to transform lives through healthcare and education. Since our inception, we have consistently worked to improve the wellbeing of residents in Turtuk Youl and Turtuk Farol, making a tangible difference in hundreds of lives.

Our Impact

Thousands of charitable donations have been made to poor and needy students and critically ill patients referred to hospitals in Leh and other states. THET has arranged patient Sarais in Disket and Leh, offering free accommodation and support facilities for patients and their families during medical treatment.

Funding & Support

THET operates without any permanent source of income. We rely on the dedication of our community, collecting annual membership fees ranging from Rs. 50 to Rs. 1000 from our trustees, and generous donations from well-wishers who believe in our mission.

Our Mission

📚 Education First

Provide financial assistance to poor and needy students, encouraging academic excellence with scholarships and rewards. Support students with essential supplies and organize career counseling sessions.

🏥 Health for All

Assist helpless patients with financial and medical support. Run hospitals, dispensaries, medical camps, and health centers to ensure quality healthcare reaches everyone.

🎯 Community Growth

Conduct seminars, workshops, and public lectures to raise awareness and build a stronger, more informed community committed to collective progress.

Our Vision

  • ✓ Achieve 100% literacy in the block and adjoining areas
